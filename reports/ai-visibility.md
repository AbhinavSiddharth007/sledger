**Overall: about 65/100 (proxy)**

## Key competitor pattern
ClickUp and monday.com are the most frequent winners in productivity and all-in-one prompts. Nuclino and Slite win the lightweight-wiki prompts. Obsidian and Apple Notes take the personal-knowledge and free-tool prompts.

## Top GEO/AEO fixes for notion.so
1. **Publish "Notion vs [X]" comparison pages** for ClickUp, Coda, monday.com and Obsidian. Use a comparison table with pricing and plan limits. Alternatives prompts are where Notion is currently named as the thing being replaced.
2. **Add a clear, plain-language "who Notion is for" summary** near the top of the homepage and pricing page. Lead with the wiki and docs use case, which is where Notion already wins.
3. **Publish current, dated pricing and plan details** in text (not only in the UI). Sources cite cost as a barrier, so publish a free-tier and team-tier comparison.
4. **Address governance and data-ownership questions** on a public page: permissions, export, admin controls and data residency. These are the lock-in concerns sources raise.
5. **Get listed and reviewed on third-party roundups and review sites** (G2, Capterra, Software Advice) with current, specific reviews. Roundups drive most of the answers.
6. **Add FAQ and Organization/SoftwareApplication schema** so assistants can extract pricing, categories and features with confidence.
